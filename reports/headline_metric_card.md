# Headline Metric Card

- **Metric:** Verified Grounded Reply Quality Score
- **Value:** **0.767 / 1.000** (95% CI: $[0.7612, 0.7728]$)
- **Baseline:** Historical Human Response Baseline = **0.500** (Generic Template Baseline = **0.265**)
- **Delta:** Absolute: **+0.267** (+0.502 vs. Generic) | Relative: **+53.4%** (+189.4% vs. Generic)
- **Dataset:** Phase 18 Frozen Synthetic End-to-End Test Cohort
- **Sample Size:** N = 200 customer support interactions
- **Definition:** Mean normalized quality rating across 6 dimensions (Relevance, Groundedness, Correctness, Helpfulness, Completeness, Style) for replies generated by the grounded LLM and audited by the post-generation verification gate.
- **Primary Strength:** Combines dense historical knowledge grounding with automated claim-verification safety, ensuring that responses both address the customer inquiry and adhere to retrieved context.
- **Primary Limitation:** Offline proxy score evaluated on synthetic single/two-turn exchanges; does not evaluate backend database execution, live multi-turn dialogue, or real customer satisfaction (CSAT).
- **Supporting Metrics:**
  1. *Intent Classification Macro F1:* **0.857** vs. 0.580 TF-IDF baseline ($\Delta = +0.277$)
  2. *Escalation Expected Cost:* **2.14** vs. 6.10 Always-Auto baseline ($\Delta = -3.96$, -64.9% cost reduction)
  3. *Grounding Verification Pass Rate:* **56.0%** (11.5% unsupported claims caught and repaired)
  4. *Critical Unsafe Auto-Handle Rate:* **2.0%** (4 high-risk failures requiring deterministic safety intervention)
- **Interpretation:** The headline score demonstrates that grounding generative models in retrieved historical context and enforcing automated claim verification substantially elevates response quality over raw historical reuse. However, this metric must never be interpreted as autonomous production readiness or guaranteed real-world resolution.
